About the platform

Building a culture of inquiry and collaboration

SWACS Conferences & Academic Events is envisioned as a central digital platform for academic programmes organised by St. Wilfred’s College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Panvel.

It brings event information into one place so students, faculty members, researchers and professionals can discover programmes, understand participation requirements and engage with academic activities.

  • Multidisciplinary and inclusive academic programming
  • Clear event information and submission guidance
  • Opportunities for research dissemination and collaboration
  • A growing archive of approved academic outputs
